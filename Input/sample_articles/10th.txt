Date: 2024-03-12

Governments and industry leaders gathered at an international summit to discuss the intersection of technology, climate policy, and economic development. Participants highlighted the role of artificial intelligence in optimizing renewable energy grids and predicting climate risks. Economists suggested that green technology investments could drive the next wave of global economic growth while reducing environmental damage. However, some experts warned that unequal access to new technologies may widen the gap between developed and developing countries. The summit concluded with calls for stronger international cooperation to address climate change while supporting sustainable economic progress.
