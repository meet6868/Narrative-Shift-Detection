Date: 2024-02-11

A new international agreement aims to accelerate investment in renewable energy technologies. Several countries pledged billions of dollars to expand solar and wind power capacity in an effort to reduce dependence on fossil fuels. Energy analysts believe the transition to clean energy could reshape global trade patterns, particularly for countries that currently rely on oil exports. Technology companies are also developing advanced battery systems to store renewable energy more efficiently. Environmental groups welcomed the announcement but cautioned that stronger climate policies will be necessary to meet long-term emissions reduction targets.
